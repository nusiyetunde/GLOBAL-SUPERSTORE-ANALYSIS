# GLOBAL-SUPERSTORE-SALES-AND-PROFIT-PERFORMANCE
## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional performance.

The goal of the project is to transform raw business data into an interactive dashboard that provides meaningful business insights and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Global Superstore Dataset

  ## Key Performance Indicator
- Total Sales: $12.64M
- Total Profit: $1.47M
- Profit Margin: 11.61%
- Average Sales: $246.49

  ## Key Business Insights
  - The company generated $12.64M in total sales and $1.47M in total profit.
- Central is the highest-performing region by sales.
- Consumer contributes the largest share of sales.
- Technology generates the highest category sales.
- Sales show an overall upward trend over time.

  ## Business Risks
- Moderate profit margin
- Uneven regional performance
- Sales and profit fluctuation overtime

  ## Business Opportunities
 - Expand technology product sales.
- Grow the Consumer customer segment.
- Improve sales performance in weaker regions.

## Recommendation
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products.
- Develop targeted strategies for the Consumer segment.
- Investigate weaker-performing regions.
- Monitor product-level profitability.
- Improve the overall profit margin.

  ## Conclusion
  The Power BI dashboard provides a clear overview of Global Superstore's sales and profitability performance. The analysis highlights strong-performing regions, categories and customer segments while identifying areas where the business can improve profitability and regional performance.
